A Legacy of Taste and Tradition

Founded on the belief that dining should be an immersive sensory journey, Restaurant Lelebotte has become a sanctuary in Douala for those who appreciate the finer details of Cameroonian and world gastronomy.

Our journey began in a small kitchen by the Cameroonian coast, where Chef Julian Lelebotte learned the secrets of simple perfection. Today we bring that same philosophy to every plate in Douala.
